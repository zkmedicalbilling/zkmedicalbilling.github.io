Appeals & Reconsideration

Learn claim corrections, payer disputes, Medicare appeals and evidence-based follow-up.

← All learning resources

Choose the right action

Rejected claim

Claim did not pass front-end edits. Fix the actual error, resubmit, confirm payer acceptance and protect filing limits.

Corrected claim

Replace an accepted claim when actual claim data need correction. Use required original claim reference and frequency indicator.

Reconsideration

Ask payer to review an adjudicated determination; commercial definitions and submission routes differ.

Formal appeal

Challenge a coverage, payment or other eligible adverse decision using the applicable appeal rights and evidence.

Reopening

Request review of certain errors under applicable rules, such as Medicare clerical errors; not the same as a formal appeal.

Grievance

Complaint about service or administration, not necessarily a claim payment dispute.

Appeal workflow

  1. Read the ERA/EOB and notice: identify claim number, CARC/RARC, denial reason, adjudication date, appeal rights and deadline.
  2. Confirm eligibility, coverage, authorization, contract, code accuracy and actual service documentation.
  3. Determine whether rejection correction, corrected claim, payment dispute, reopening or formal appeal is appropriate.
  4. Collect the original claim, remittance, relevant clinical notes, authorization, payer policy and proof of timely submission as applicable.
  5. Write a concise factual request explaining the disputed decision, evidence and requested remedy. Use secure approved channels.
  6. Save receipt, appeal tracking number, deadline and follow-up date. Monitor outcome; escalate only via the applicable next level.
  7. Post the final decision and document patient responsibility only when supported by the remittance, contract and law.

Original Medicare FFS — five levels

LevelReview body / filing window
1. RedeterminationMAC; generally 120 days after receipt of the initial determination.
2. ReconsiderationQIC; generally 180 days after receipt of the redetermination.
3. Hearing / reviewOMHA; applicable amount-in-controversy and filing requirements.
4. Council reviewMedicare Appeals Council; follow the prior decision notice.
5. Judicial reviewFederal district court; jurisdiction and amount-in-controversy requirements apply.

These are Original Medicare FFS stages, not universal commercial rules. Medicare Advantage enrollee reconsideration generally must be requested within 65 calendar days from the organization determination notice; provider payment disputes may follow different procedures. Medicare FFS late-filing denials generally are not appealable initial determinations; check limited filing exceptions.

Practical denial scenarios

ReasonEvidence / route
Medical necessityClinical records and applicable coverage policy; request medical review.
No authorizationApproval record or permitted retrospective process and payer notice.
Timely filingPayer acceptance report with claim ID, dates, and applicable exception evidence; first check appeal rights.
Duplicate claimCompare prior adjudication and claim identifiers; do not blindly resubmit.
UnderpaymentContracted rate, units, fee schedule and remittance calculation; use contractual dispute process.
COB / wrong payerVerify primary and secondary order and correct claim destination.
Non-covered serviceCheck plan exclusions, notice requirements and correct coding; do not automatically bill patient.

Sample reconsideration letter

To: Payer Claims Review Department
Subject: Request for claim reconsideration

Provider: [Provider name / NPI]
Member ID: [Use secure channel only]
Claim number: [Claim reference]
Date of service: [DOS]
Denial reason: [CARC/RARC and explanation]

We request review of the above determination. The claim was denied for [reason]. Our records show [verified facts]. Enclosed are [relevant documents]. Based on [applicable policy or contract], please review the determination and reprocess the claim if appropriate.

Please provide the written decision and reference number.

Sincerely,
[Authorized billing representative]

Do not submit a template without tailoring it to the verified denial facts and authorized process.
