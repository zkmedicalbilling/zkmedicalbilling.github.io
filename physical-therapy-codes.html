Physical Therapy Codes & Billing

A practical US outpatient PT billing guide: common CPT/HCPCS code families, documentation, timed units, modifiers, and payer checks.

Scope & accuracy

This is an educational quick reference, not a complete licensed CPT codebook. Code descriptions below are plain-language topic labels, not official CPT descriptors. Confirm the current code set, date-of-service rules, payer policy, provider qualifications, and documentation before billing.

ICD-10-CM Diagnosis Codes Commonly Used in Physical Therapy

Search a practical selection of diagnosis codes frequently encountered in outpatient PT. There is no separate, closed “Physical Therapy ICD-10 code set”: report the diagnosis, condition, symptom, or reason for the encounter supported by the medical record. This is a curated reference, not an exhaustive list of every ICD-10-CM code that could ever be relevant to PT.

FY 2027 code-year reminder

For dates of service October 1, 2026 through September 30, 2027, verify each complete code, descriptor, inclusion/exclusion note, required 7th character, and billable status in the official FY 2027 ICD-10-CM Tabular List and Index. A code family or parent category may not be a billable claim code.

Billing essentials

01 / Timed servicesCount only qualifying treatment time

For Medicare outpatient therapy, apply CMS unit methodology to total timed minutes for the discipline and date of service. Do not count waiting, rest, independent exercise, or untimed services as timed minutes. Commercial plans may use different rules.

02 / DocumentationShow skilled, medically necessary care

Support the diagnosis, functional limitations, measurable goals, skilled interventions, response/progress, plan of care, and required certification or recertification. Record time by service when required.

03 / ModifiersCheck discipline and assistant modifiers

GP commonly identifies services under an outpatient physical therapy plan of care for Medicare. CQ may apply to qualifying PTA-furnished portions under Medicare rules. Verify current CMS and payer instructions.

04 / Edits & paymentReview bundling and payer edits

Check current NCCI procedure-to-procedure edits, medically unlikely edits, MPPR, authorization, visit limits, referrals, place of service, and contract terms. A modifier does not automatically override an edit.

Medicare reference points — CY 2026

$2,480PT + SLP combined KX threshold for CY 2026
$3,000Targeted medical review threshold reference for PT/SLP
8 minutesMinimum time for a single 15-minute timed unit in the applicable Medicare methodology

KX threshold is not a hard therapy cap. KX indicates that continued services meet applicable medical-necessity requirements and are supported by the record. Thresholds and policies can change annually.
