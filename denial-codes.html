EOB and ERA code types

CARCClaim adjustment reasons

Explains why a claim or service amount changed. Not every adjustment is a denial.

RARCRemittance remarks

Provides additional explanation or an informational alert. Read it alongside the CARC.

CO / PR / OA / PIAdjustment group

Identifies the adjustment category. Verify liability before posting or collecting.

PLBProvider-level balance

Reconcile offsets and other provider-level transactions separately from claim adjustments.

Payer-specific AR workflow

Original Medicare (MCR)Part A / Part B

Check MAC, eligibility/MSP, LCD/NCD, NCCI edits, applicable ABN and QMB status. Use corrected claim, reopening or redetermination as appropriate. Never collect Medicare cost-sharing from a QMB beneficiary.

CMS remittance guidance ↗
Medicaid (MCD)State FFS / MCO

Verify state program, managed care enrollment, provider participation, prior authorization and third-party liability. Follow the state or MCO deadline and balance-billing restrictions. Preserve the primary payer adjustment codes on secondary submissions.

Medicaid program resources ↗ · MassHealth EOB code lists ↗ · New York Medicaid ↗
CommercialHMO / PPO / EPO / POS

Verify product, network, referral, authorization, benefits, contract and filing limits. Use payer-specific corrected claim or appeal instructions. Confirm patient liability under contract and applicable law.

Medicare AdvantagePart C / Supplemental

For Medicare Advantage use the plan's provider payment dispute or appeal process. For Medigap verify Medicare crossover and supplemental benefits. QMB protections apply regardless of Medicare delivery model.

Common EOB examples and action

CO-16 + N290

Investigate rendering provider identifier. Validate actual NPI and enrollment before correction.

CO-50 + N115

Review the applicable LCD and supporting records; appeal if criteria are satisfied.

PR-1 / PR-2 / PR-3

Review deductible, coinsurance and copay, secondary coverage and QMB status before collection.

CO-119

Verify benefit limits and secondary plan requirements; do not assume a contractual adjustment becomes patient liability.
