Patient Insurance Plans & Network Types

Learn how common US health plan designs affect provider networks, referrals, out-of-network benefits, authorizations, and patient responsibility.

Before every claim

Plan type is only a starting point. Benefits vary by employer/group, state, product, network, funding arrangement, and Evidence of Coverage. Verify active coverage, exact product/network, provider status, service coverage, referral, prior authorization, deductible, copay, coinsurance, visit limits, and exclusions for the date of service.

Important terms on an insurance card or eligibility response

DeductibleAmount member pays first

What the member must pay for covered services before the plan begins paying under the benefit, subject to exceptions.

CopaymentFixed member amount

A set dollar amount for a covered service, such as a visit, when the benefit specifies one.

CoinsurancePercentage share

The member's percentage of the plan's allowed amount after applicable deductible and benefit rules.

Out-of-pocket maximumAnnual cost-sharing limit

A limit on covered in-network cost sharing under applicable plan rules; premiums and some non-covered/OON costs may not count.

PCP / ReferralPrimary care coordination

Some products require selection of a primary care provider and/or a referral before specialist services.

Prior authorizationAdvance medical review

Approval may be required before specified services. Authorization is not a guarantee of payment or coverage.

Medicare and Medicaid plan note

Medicare Advantage includes different plan designs, such as HMO and PPO, with different network and referral rules. Medicaid managed-care benefits and networks vary by state and plan. Always use the member's current Evidence of Coverage, Summary of Benefits, state program guidance, and payer portal response.
