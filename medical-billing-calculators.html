10 interactive billing calculators

Change the example inputs and select Calculate. All calculations run locally in your browser; no patient data is required.

Patient responsibility estimate

Assumes covered in-network service, deductible first, then coinsurance; optional copay applies to remaining allowed amount. Remaining OOP limit caps eligible cost sharing. Does not model every benefit design.

Enter values to calculate.

Contractual adjustment / balance

For a simple covered contracted claim. Negative residual indicates possible overpayment or input inconsistency. Not a determination of patient liability.

Enter values to calculate.

Insurance underpayment

Expected payer amount less actual payer payment; use contract and final EOB. Excludes other adjustments.

Enter values to calculate.

Timely filing & appeal deadline

Enter the correct trigger date and payer-defined calendar-day window. Original Medicare initial claim filing is generally one calendar year, not a fixed 365 days. Check leap-day, holiday, receipt and exception rules.

Enter values to calculate.

A/R aging

Days elapsed between date of service (or your organization's defined start date) and as-of date. Aging bucket follows common 30-day reporting convention.

Enter values to calculate.

Denial rate

Denied claims divided by adjudicated claims for the same period and population; use a consistent claim-level definition.

Enter values to calculate.

Clean claim rate

Claims meeting your first-pass clean-claim definition divided by claims submitted; use consistent counting.

Enter values to calculate.

Net collection rate

Payments net of refunds divided by charges less contractual adjustments, using aligned periods or claim cohorts. Not gross collection rate.

Enter values to calculate.

Days in A/R

Total outstanding A/R divided by average daily charges (or net revenue, per your consistent reporting methodology).

Enter values to calculate.

Remaining authorized visits / units

Use the same measurement unit for all inputs. Payer benefit limits and authorization limits are separate.
