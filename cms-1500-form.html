CMS-1500 Claim Form: Box-by-Box Guide

Understand the purpose of Items 1–33, the six service lines in Item 24, and practical claim-submission checks.

What is the CMS-1500?

The CMS-1500 (02/12) is the standard paper claim form used by non-institutional providers and suppliers for professional services. The NUCC maintains the form and its general reference instructions. Medicare paper-claim instructions are in CMS Medicare Claims Processing Manual, Chapter 26. Most covered entities submit professional claims electronically using the 837P transaction; paper submission to Medicare is generally limited to qualifying exceptions.

Important before you fill any box

This is an educational quick reference, not a replacement for the current NUCC 1500 Reference Instruction Manual, payer companion guide, contract, or CMS policy. Some fields have different instructions for Medicare, Medicaid, commercial plans, workers' compensation, and other programs. A field described as reserved or not required by Medicare may still have a different payer-specific instruction. Never populate a field based only on a generic example.

Find a box

33 items and service-line fields

PATIENT / INSURANCE

Box 1 — Insurance Type

Check the coverage category applicable to the claim, such as Medicare, Medicaid, TRICARE, CHAMPVA, group health plan, FECA/Black Lung, or Other.

Check: Select the correct payer/program category; do not choose Medicare merely because the patient has a Medicare card if another payer is primary.

PATIENT / INSURANCE

Box 1a — Insured’s ID Number

Enter the identification number assigned by the payer selected in Box 1. For Medicare, use the beneficiary’s Medicare Beneficiary Identifier (MBI), not an old HICN unless an applicable exception instructs otherwise.

Check: Match the ID to the correct member and payer.

PATIENT / INSURANCE

Box 2 — Patient’s Name

Enter the patient’s legal/insurance-record name: last name, first name, and middle initial when applicable.

Check: Match the insurance card and eligibility record; avoid transposed names.

PATIENT / INSURANCE

Box 3 — Patient’s Birth Date and Sex

Enter date of birth and the sex field as requested by the form/payer. Follow the required date format.

Check: Use the member record and payer rules for demographic fields.

PATIENT / INSURANCE

Box 4 — Insured’s Name

Enter the policyholder/subscriber name when different from the patient. If patient and insured are the same, the form instructions may permit “SAME.”

Check: Use the subscriber details, not automatically the patient’s details.

PATIENT / INSURANCE

Box 5 — Patient’s Address

Enter the patient’s mailing street address, city, state, ZIP code, and telephone number as applicable.

Check: Confirm current address and payer-required formatting.

PATIENT / INSURANCE

Box 6 — Patient Relationship to Insured

Select Self, Spouse, Child, or Other, based on the patient’s relationship to the subscriber/insured.

Check: If the patient is the subscriber, select Self.

PATIENT / INSURANCE

Box 7 — Insured’s Address

Enter the insured/subscriber’s address and phone number when required. If it is the same as the patient’s, “SAME” may be used where permitted.

Check: Do not assume the subscriber lives at the patient’s address.

RESERVED

Box 8 — Reserved for NUCC Use

Reserved field on the current 02/12 form. Do not enter routine patient information here.

Note: Do not confuse this with older 08/05 form instructions.

OTHER COVERAGE

Box 9 — Other Insured’s Name

Used for other insured/supplemental coverage information when applicable. Medicare’s instructions have specific Medigap-related use and restrictions for participating providers.

Check: Follow the payer and current NUCC instructions; do not place unrelated secondary-plan details here by assumption.

OTHER COVERAGE

Box 9a — Other Insured’s Policy / Group Number

Enter the applicable other-insured policy or group number when the field is being used under the relevant instructions. Medicare has specific Medigap formatting rules.

RESERVED

Box 9b — Reserved for NUCC Use

Reserved on the 02/12 form. Do not use for routine data unless an authoritative current instruction specifically directs otherwise.

RESERVED

Box 9c — Reserved for NUCC Use

Reserved on the 02/12 form. Do not enter a payer address or other data here unless the current form instructions for the applicable program expressly require it.

OTHER COVERAGE

Box 9d — Insurance Plan / Program Name

Enter the plan or program name when required for the applicable other-insurance/Medigap use. Medicare has specific COBA Medigap identifier instructions.

CONDITION / COB

Box 10a — Employment Related?

Indicate whether the patient’s condition is related to current or previous employment, using Yes or No.

Billing impact: A Yes response may indicate workers’ compensation or other coverage should be investigated.

CONDITION / COB

Box 10b — Auto Accident?

Indicate whether the condition is related to an auto accident. If Yes, enter the state where the accident occurred as required.

CONDITION / COB

Box 10c — Other Accident?

Indicate whether the condition is related to another type of accident, such as a non-auto accident.

CONDITION / COB

Box 10d — Claim Codes

Use for claim-code information designated by NUCC or the payer. CMS Medicare instructions specifically direct Medicaid-related information here in certain Medicare claim circumstances; do not assume that Medicare-specific use applies to every payer.

OTHER COVERAGE

Box 11 — Insured’s Policy / Group Number

Enter the insured’s policy/group number when another insurance is primary to Medicare. Medicare requires this field and instructs providers to enter “NONE” when no insurance is primary to Medicare.

Check: Make a good-faith Medicare Secondary Payer (MSP) inquiry where applicable.

OTHER COVERAGE

Box 11a — Insured’s Date of Birth / Sex

Enter the insured’s date of birth and sex when different from the patient’s information in Box 3.

OTHER COVERAGE

Box 11b — Other Claim ID

Enter the applicable other-claim identifier or qualifier information as directed by the current NUCC manual or payer. Medicare may have distinct instructions for this field.

OTHER COVERAGE

Box 11c — Insurance Plan / Program Name

Enter the primary insurer’s plan/program name or payer identifier as directed. Medicare instructions require the primary payer PAYERID or complete program/plan name when other insurance is primary to Medicare.

OTHER COVERAGE

Box 11d — Is There Another Health Benefit Plan?

Indicate whether another health benefit plan may cover the patient, following the form’s instructions and payer requirements.

Important: Medicare’s Chapter 26 says this item is not required by Medicare; other payer workflows may differ.

AUTHORIZATION

Box 12 — Patient’s / Authorized Person’s Signature

Patient or authorized representative signature (or “Signature on File” where permitted) authorizes release of information needed to process the claim.

Check: Maintain the underlying signed authorization in the record when required.

AUTHORIZATION

Box 13 — Insured’s / Authorized Person’s Signature

Authorizes payment of medical benefits to the provider/supplier when applicable. “Signature on File” may be used when properly supported and permitted.

Check: Do not treat Box 12 and Box 13 as interchangeable; they authorize different things.

ILLNESS / PROVIDER

Box 14 — Date of Current Illness / Injury / Pregnancy

Enter the relevant onset/current-illness, injury, or pregnancy date when applicable. Use the appropriate date format and qualifier if required by the form/payer.

ILLNESS / PROVIDER

Box 15 — Other Date

Used for another date and its qualifier when applicable, according to the current form and payer instructions. Medicare Chapter 26 generally instructs providers to leave this item blank.

ILLNESS / PROVIDER

Box 16 — Dates Unable to Work

Enter the dates the patient is unable to work in their current occupation when applicable.

PROVIDER DETAILS

Box 17 — Name of Referring / Ordering / Supervising Provider

Enter the applicable provider’s name and role qualifier: DN (referring), DK (ordering), or DQ (supervising), as appropriate to the claim and payer rules.

PT note: Medicare therapy claims have specific requirements for the certifying provider’s name in Box 17.

PROVIDER DETAILS

Box 17a — Other ID

Used for an applicable non-NPI identifier only when permitted by the current instructions. Medicare Chapter 26 directs providers not to report 17a.

PROVIDER DETAILS

Box 17b — Referring / Ordering / Supervising NPI

Enter the NPI of the provider named in Box 17 when required.

PT note: Medicare requires the certifying physician/nonphysician practitioner’s NPI in 17b for therapy plan-of-care claims.

CLAIM DETAILS

Box 18 — Hospitalization Dates

Enter the from/to dates of a hospitalization related to the services being billed, when applicable.

CLAIM DETAILS

Box 19 — Additional Claim Information

Use for additional information required by the payer or NUCC instructions. Medicare may require a concise description for certain unlisted/NOC codes when space permits, or an attachment.

CLAIM DETAILS

Box 20 — Outside Lab?

Indicate whether an outside laboratory performed the service and enter charges where required. Medicare uses this field for diagnostic tests subject to the anti-markup payment limitation.

DIAGNOSIS

Box 21 — Diagnosis / Nature of Illness or Injury

Enter diagnosis codes to the highest supported specificity. The 02/12 form provides up to 12 diagnosis positions (A–L); select the applicable ICD indicator and link each service line to the appropriate diagnosis in 24E.

Check: Diagnosis must be supported by documentation and valid for the date of service.

CLAIM DETAILS

Box 22 — Resubmission Code / Original Reference Number

Used for claim resubmission/correction information when required by the payer, including the original payer claim control number. Medicare Chapter 26 says this field is not required by Medicare.

Check: Follow the payer’s corrected-claim frequency code and original claim number instructions.

CLAIM DETAILS

Box 23 — Prior Authorization Number

Enter the authorization/referral number or another identifier specifically requested for the service. Medicare may use this field for certain QIO approvals, IDE numbers, CLIA numbers, or other defined information.

Check: Authorization does not guarantee coverage or payment.

Box 24 — Service Line Details (24A–24J)

There are six service lines on the CMS-1500. Each line reports a service, procedure, supply, or drug detail. Do not interpret the shaded supplemental area as six additional service lines.

24A

Date(s) of Service

Enter the date the service was performed. Use From/To dates when allowed and appropriate. For a multi-day span, follow payer rules and ensure a valid To date when required.

24B

Place of Service (POS)

Enter the two-digit POS code identifying where the service was furnished (for example, office, home, or hospital outpatient setting). Use the correct code for the actual service location and payer rules.

24C

EMG — Emergency Indicator

Mark when the service meets the payer’s definition of emergency. Medicare does not require this field; do not mark it simply because the visit was urgent or same-day.

24D

Procedure / Service / Modifier

Enter the applicable CPT or HCPCS code and supported modifiers. The form accommodates up to four modifiers. Report only codes/modifiers supported by documentation and applicable coding rules.

24E

Diagnosis Pointer

Enter the letter (A–L) that links this service line to the diagnosis in Box 21. Use one primary pointer per line on the paper form, following payer instructions.

24F

Charges

Enter the charge for the individual service line, not the total claim charge.

24G

Days or Units

Enter the number of units, visits, or services billed. For timed therapy services, calculate units using the applicable payer methodology and documented treatment time; do not assume every payer uses the same rule.

24H

EPSDT / Family Planning

Enter the applicable EPSDT or family-planning indicator when required for the claim/program. Medicare does not require this field.

24I

ID Qualifier

Enter the qualifier that identifies the type of rendering-provider identifier reported in 24J, when required. Medicare does not require this field.

24J

Rendering Provider ID / NPI

Enter the rendering provider’s NPI in the lower, unshaded portion when required. The shaded portion is for supplemental information and is not the rendering NPI field under Medicare instructions.

Boxes 25–33 — Billing and Provider Information

BILLING DETAILS

Box 25 — Federal Tax ID Number

Enter the billing provider/supplier’s EIN or SSN and check the correct type. Ensure it matches enrollment and payer records.

BILLING DETAILS

Box 26 — Patient Account Number

Enter the provider’s internal patient/account identifier. This is mainly for claim tracking and reconciliation; avoid putting sensitive unrelated information here.

BILLING DETAILS

Box 27 — Accept Assignment?

Indicate whether the provider accepts assignment of benefits for the claim. Medicare has services and provider categories that must be paid on an assignment basis.

BILLING DETAILS

Box 28 — Total Charge

Enter the total billed charges. It should equal the sum of the individual service-line charges in 24F.

BILLING DETAILS

Box 29 — Amount Paid

Enter the amount already paid by the patient toward the covered services, as applicable. Do not enter payer payments here unless the payer’s instructions specifically direct otherwise.

RESERVED

Box 30 — Reserved for NUCC Use

Reserved on the current 02/12 form. Do not enter routine balance or payment information here.

SIGNATURE

Box 31 — Provider Signature / Date

Provider/supplier or authorized representative signature and date. “Signature on File” or an approved electronic/computer-generated signature may be accepted when the applicable rules and supporting records are satisfied.

SERVICE LOCATION

Box 32 — Service Facility Location

Enter the name, address, and ZIP code of the location where services were rendered when required. Do not automatically copy the billing address if the service occurred elsewhere.

BILLING PROVIDER

Box 33 — Billing Provider Information & Phone

Enter billing provider/supplier name, address, ZIP code, and telephone number. This identifies the entity submitting the claim and receiving payer correspondence.

NPI

Box 32a — Service Facility NPI

Enter the service facility NPI when required by payer/program instructions.

PAYER-SPECIFIC

Box 32b — Other ID

Use only when a payer/program-specific instruction requires an additional identifier. Medicare generally says not to report this item.

NPI

Box 33a — Billing Provider NPI

Enter the NPI of the billing provider/group. Verify the NPI is enrolled/credentialed with the payer for the billed service and location.

PAYER-SPECIFIC

Box 33b — Other ID

Generally not reported for Medicare unless a specific policy instruction requires it. Follow the payer’s current companion guide.

Quick claim review checklist

  • Patient name, DOB, member ID, and payer selection match eligibility.
  • Subscriber/insured details and relationship are accurate.
  • Other coverage, accident, and MSP questions are answered correctly.
  • Diagnosis codes in Box 21 are valid for the date of service and supported by documentation.
  • Each Box 24 service line has correct DOS, POS, CPT/HCPCS, modifiers, diagnosis pointer, charge, units, and rendering provider details.
  • Service-line charges in 24F add up to Box 28 total charges.
  • Billing provider, tax ID, NPI, and service-facility details match payer enrollment records.
  • Authorization, referral, medical records, and attachments are included when required.
  • For corrected claims, use the payer’s required resubmission method and original claim reference.
Important distinction: paper CMS-1500 vs electronic 837P

The CMS-1500 is the paper form. Electronic professional claims use the HIPAA 837P transaction and its implementation guide. Electronic data loops/segments do not always map one-to-one to a printed box, so do not use this page as an 837P mapping specification.

Official references

  • CMS — Professional Paper Claim Form (CMS-1500)
  • CMS — Medicare Claims Processing Manual, Chapter 26 (current online revision)
  • NUCC — 1500 Claim Form and Reference Instruction Manual

CMS Chapter 26 is Medicare-specific. NUCC’s manual provides general 1500 form instructions and is updated periodically. Always check the latest available manual and payer-specific requirements before submitting a claim.
