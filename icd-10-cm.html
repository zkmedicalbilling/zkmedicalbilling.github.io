Important ICD-10-CM coding notice

Educational code-description directory; not a substitute for the official Tabular List and Alphabetic Index. Code validity, billable specificity, sequencing, laterality, encounter character, exclusions, and documentation requirements must be verified in the official FY 2027 Tabular List and Alphabetic Index for the date of service. Do not assign a diagnosis based only on a symptom or billing preference. The provider’s documentation and official coding guidelines control.
