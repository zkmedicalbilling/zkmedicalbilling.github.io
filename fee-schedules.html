How to check a rate

Example: CPT 99213. For Medicare select year, locality, modifier and non-facility or facility amount. For Medicaid open the physician medicine schedule and find the code, applicable modifier and effective date. A fee schedule amount is not necessarily the actual claim payment; coverage, deductible, coinsurance, units and adjustments may apply.
