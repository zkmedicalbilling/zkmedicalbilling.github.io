First Health / inactive TPA — practical A/R scenario

  1. Check eligibility on the original DOS, not only current coverage, and read both sides of the card.
  2. Identify the plan sponsor, insurer or TPA responsible for that DOS; confirm whether the former TPA still handles run-out claims.
  3. If First Health cannot resolve a claim because the named TPA is inactive, ask for the responsible payer's contact or successor administrator if available.
  4. If unresolved, request updated insurance and historical coverage details from the patient through approved secure channels; contact the employer/plan administrator if authorized.
  5. Document reference numbers and protect filing/dispute deadlines. Do not automatically transfer to patient responsibility because a TPA is inactive.

What to verify before claim submission

Member ID and group · DOS eligibility · plan and network · payer ID and claim address · claims department · prior authorization vendor · timely filing and appeal window · COB · call reference. Use the ID card, payer manual, portal and your clearinghouse; no universal payer ID or mailing address applies to every product.
