A/R & Denial Scenarios

Learn accounts receivable follow-up, unpaid claim scenarios, denials, appeals, and account resolution.

What is Accounts Receivable?

AR is the outstanding amount owed for services already provided. Prioritize by aging, balance, filing and appeal deadlines. Document payer contact, reference, findings and next action.

Educational examples only. Verify current payer rules and contracts. Never enter patient-identifiable information here. Sample notes use placeholders, not actual claim findings.

Original A/R overview (English)

Accounts Receivable (AR) is the money a healthcare provider is owed for services already provided but not yet fully paid. In medical billing, AR commonly includes balances pending with insurance payers and, where appropriate, amounts owed by patients.

Why AR follow-up matters

Regular AR follow-up helps identify delayed, unpaid, underpaid, or incorrectly processed claims. The goal is to resolve each account accurately, document every action, and follow payer deadlines and applicable rules.

Common AR scenarios and follow-up actions

  1. Claim not received by payer: Check clearinghouse reports and payer claim status. If there is no payer receipt, correct any transmission issue and resubmit according to payer instructions. Keep proof of submission.
  2. Claim received but still processing: Confirm the claim is on file, ask whether additional information is needed, record the reference number and expected processing timeframe, then follow up if it exceeds that timeframe.
  3. Claim denied: Review the EOB or ERA and identify the exact denial reason. Check eligibility, authorization, coding, documentation, timely filing, and payer policy as relevant. Correct and resubmit or appeal when supported and permitted.
  4. Claim partially paid or underpaid: Compare the allowed amount, payment, adjustments, and patient responsibility with the contract and EOB/ERA. Investigate discrepancies and submit a reconsideration or appeal when appropriate.
  5. Primary insurance paid or denied; secondary claim pending: Verify whether the primary EOB/ERA was forwarded to the secondary payer. If not, submit the secondary claim with the required primary remittance information and supporting documents, following coordination-of-benefits rules.
  6. Medical records requested: Confirm the request is valid and identify the records and deadline required. Submit only the necessary, authorized documentation through an approved secure channel and retain confirmation. Delayed or missing records may affect claim payment.
  7. Authorization or referral issue: Review the payer's denial and the authorization/referral record for the date and service. Request a correction or retro-authorization only if payer policy allows; otherwise follow the applicable appeal or contractual process.
  8. Timely filing denial: Review the payer's filing limit and available submission evidence. If the claim was submitted on time, send a supported appeal with proof. Do not assume a claim can be reopened without checking payer rules.
  9. Patient responsibility balance: Review the final EOB/ERA, benefits, contract, and applicable patient-billing rules before billing the patient. Do not transfer an amount to the patient solely because a claim was denied.
  10. Credit balance or overpayment: Verify the payment history and determine whether a refund, recoupment, or adjustment is required. Follow provider policy, payer instructions, and applicable regulations, and document the resolution.

Good AR work habits

  • Prioritize by aging, balance, filing or appeal deadline, and risk.
  • Check payer portals and clearinghouse status before calling.
  • Record call date, representative name, reference number, findings, and next action.
  • Set a realistic follow-up date and track the account until resolution.
  • Protect patient information and use approved secure systems.

Important: These are general educational examples. Actual next steps depend on the EOB/ERA, payer policy, provider contract, claim type, and applicable law. Never alter or submit claim information without appropriate documentation and authorization.

اصل تعارف (اردو)

Accounts Receivable (AR) سے مراد وہ رقم ہے جو healthcare provider کو فراہم کی گئی خدمات کے بدلے وصول ہونا باقی ہو۔ Medical billing میں اس میں عموماً insurance payer کے پاس pending balances اور مناسب صورت میں مریض کے ذمے واجب الادا رقم شامل ہوتی ہے۔

AR Follow-Up کیوں ضروری ہے؟

باقاعدہ AR follow-up سے تاخیر کا شکار، unpaid، underpaid یا غلط process ہونے والے claims کی نشاندہی ہوتی ہے۔ مقصد ہر اکاؤنٹ کو درست طریقے سے حل کرنا، کارروائی کا ریکارڈ رکھنا اور payer کی deadlines پر عمل کرنا ہے۔

عام AR Scenarios اور ان پر کارروائی

  1. Payer کو کلیم موصول نہیں ہوا: Clearinghouse reports اور payer claim status چیک کریں۔ اگر payer کو کلیم نہیں ملا تو transmission کا مسئلہ درست کر کے payer کی ہدایات کے مطابق دوبارہ جمع کروائیں اور submission کا ثبوت محفوظ رکھیں۔
  2. کلیم موصول ہوا مگر ابھی process ہو رہا ہے: تصدیق کریں کہ کلیم payer کے پاس موجود ہے یا نہیں، معلوم کریں کہ مزید معلومات درکار ہیں یا نہیں، reference number اور متوقع وقت نوٹ کریں، اور مقررہ وقت گزرنے پر follow-up کریں۔
  3. Claim Denied: EOB یا ERA دیکھ کر denial کی اصل وجہ معلوم کریں۔ ضرورت کے مطابق eligibility، authorization، coding، documentation، timely filing اور payer policy چیک کریں۔ درستگی کے بعد resubmit کریں یا مناسب ثبوت کے ساتھ appeal کریں۔
  4. Partial Payment یا Underpayment: Allowed amount، payment، adjustments اور patient responsibility کو contract اور EOB/ERA سے compare کریں۔ فرق کی تحقیق کریں اور مناسب صورت میں reconsideration یا appeal جمع کروائیں۔
  5. Primary نے payment یا denial دی، Secondary claim pending ہے: چیک کریں کہ primary EOB/ERA secondary payer کو forward ہوئی تھی یا نہیں۔ اگر نہیں ہوئی تو coordination-of-benefits rules کے مطابق ضروری primary remittance information اور supporting documents کے ساتھ secondary claim جمع کروائیں۔
  6. Medical Records کی درخواست: درخواست کی تصدیق کریں، مطلوبہ records اور آخری تاریخ معلوم کریں۔ صرف ضروری اور مجاز records approved secure channel سے بھیجیں اور submission confirmation محفوظ رکھیں۔ تاخیر یا records نہ بھیجنے سے payment متاثر ہو سکتی ہے۔
  7. Authorization یا Referral کا مسئلہ: Denial اور متعلقہ تاریخ و سروس کے authorization/referral record کا جائزہ لیں۔ Correction یا retro-authorization صرف اسی صورت میں مانگیں جب payer policy اجازت دیتی ہو؛ ورنہ متعلقہ appeal یا contractual process اختیار کریں۔
  8. Timely Filing Denial: Payer کی filing limit اور submission evidence چیک کریں۔ اگر کلیم وقت پر جمع ہوا تھا تو ثبوت کے ساتھ appeal کریں۔ Payer rules دیکھے بغیر یہ فرض نہ کریں کہ کلیم دوبارہ کھولا جا سکتا ہے۔
  9. Patient Responsibility Balance: مریض کو بل بھیجنے سے پہلے final EOB/ERA، benefits، contract اور مریض سے billing کے متعلقہ قواعد چیک کریں۔ صرف denial ہونے کی وجہ سے رقم مریض پر منتقل نہ کریں۔
  10. Credit Balance یا Overpayment: Payment history verify کریں اور معلوم کریں کہ refund، recoupment یا adjustment درکار ہے یا نہیں۔ Provider policy، payer instructions اور متعلقہ قوانین کے مطابق کارروائی کریں اور resolution کا نوٹ رکھیں۔

اچھی AR Follow-Up عادات

  • Aging، balance، filing یا appeal deadline اور risk کے مطابق کام کو ترجیح دیں۔
  • Call کرنے سے پہلے payer portal اور clearinghouse status چیک کریں۔
  • Call date، representative name، reference number، جواب اور next action درج کریں۔
  • Follow-up date مقرر کریں اور اکاؤنٹ کے حل ہونے تک tracking جاری رکھیں۔
  • مریض کی معلومات محفوظ رکھیں اور صرف منظور شدہ secure systems استعمال کریں۔

اہم نوٹ: یہ عمومی تعلیمی مثالیں ہیں۔ اصل کارروائی EOB/ERA، payer policy، provider contract، claim type اور متعلقہ قانون کے مطابق ہوگی۔ مناسب documentation اور authorization کے بغیر claim information تبدیل یا submit نہ کریں۔

01Claim not receivedClaim missing from payer system+

Clearinghouse اور payer acceptance چیک کریں۔ درست payer ID کے ساتھ قواعد کے مطابق کلیم جمع کریں اور ثبوت محفوظ رکھیں۔

Possible causes

Transmission rejection, incorrect payer ID or routing failure.

Verification checklist

Review clearinghouse acceptance and payer 277CA; verify payer ID, DOS and claim destination.

Resolution / next action

If no accepted claim exists, correct the routing or rejection and submit as an original claim per payer rules. Retain acceptance evidence.

Follow-up

Check payer receipt and claim number after the payer's stated intake period.

Sample A/R note (illustrative)

Payer reports no claim on file. Reviewed submission reports; [finding]. Claim [action] per payer instructions. Follow-up [date].

02Claim pending / in processAccepted but not finalized+

کلیم نمبر، pending وجہ اور متوقع وقت معلوم کریں۔ ضرورت کے مطابق معلومات فراہم کریں۔

Possible causes

Normal adjudication, manual review or missing information.

Verification checklist

Confirm receipt date, claim number, status, pending reason and required documents.

Resolution / next action

Supply requested information securely if needed; avoid duplicate submissions while claim is processing.

Follow-up

Record expected completion date and escalate if overdue.

Sample A/R note (illustrative)

Claim on file under [claim number], pending [reason]. Rep advised [timeframe]. Ref [reference]. Follow-up [date].

03Claim deniedAdjudicated with denial+

EOB، CARC اور RARC دیکھیں۔ اصل وجہ کے مطابق درستگی یا اپیل کریں۔

Possible causes

Eligibility, coding, benefits, authorization or other payer edits.

Verification checklist

Review EOB/ERA group, CARC and RARC, line-level details, coverage and applicable policy.

Resolution / next action

Correct only documented errors; use corrected claim, reconsideration or appeal as applicable. Obtain coding review if necessary.

Follow-up

Track acceptance or appeal receipt and deadline.

Sample A/R note (illustrative)

EOB reviewed; denial [code/reason]. Verified [finding]. Referred/submitted [action]. Follow-up [date].

04Underpayment / partial paymentPayment below expected allowed amount+

معاہدے اور EOB کے مطابق allowed amount اور payment کا فرق معلوم کریں۔ مناسب dispute جمع کریں۔

Possible causes

Contract rate discrepancy, bundling, benefit limits or incorrect adjustment.

Verification checklist

Compare contract fee schedule, allowed amount, payment, adjustments, patient share and COB.

Resolution / next action

Calculate supported variance; request payment review or appeal with relevant contract and remittance evidence.

Follow-up

Track reprocessing and reconcile any additional payment.

Sample A/R note (illustrative)

Expected allowed [amount]; actual [amount]. Variance [amount] reviewed. Payment dispute [status].

05Secondary claim pendingPrimary adjudicated; secondary unresolved+

Primary EOB، crossover اور COB چیک کریں۔ ضرورت ہو تو مطلوبہ remittance کے ساتھ secondary claim بھیجیں۔

Possible causes

Missing crossover, primary remittance or COB mismatch.

Verification checklist

Check primary EOB, crossover status, secondary coverage, COB order and secondary filing deadline.

Resolution / next action

If not forwarded, submit secondary claim with required primary adjudication details; avoid duplicate crossover claims.

Follow-up

Confirm secondary receipt and final adjudication before assigning patient balance.

Sample A/R note (illustrative)

Primary EOB reviewed; crossover [status]. Secondary claim [action] with required remittance. Follow-up [date].

06Medical records requestedPre-payment or post-payment review+

درخواست، مطلوبہ ریکارڈ اور آخری تاریخ verify کریں۔ مجاز محفوظ طریقے سے ریکارڈ بھیجیں۔

Possible causes

Payer needs documentation for medical necessity, coding or audit.

Verification checklist

Verify request authenticity, claim scope, required records, deadline and secure submission channel.

Resolution / next action

Obtain complete authorized records, check DOS/provider match, submit securely and retain confirmation. Escalate incomplete records.

Follow-up

Track review outcome and any recoupment or appeal deadline.

Sample A/R note (illustrative)

MR request verified for [scope]. Records [submitted/requested] via approved channel. Confirmation [ref]. Follow-up [date].

07Authorization / referral denialRequired authorization or referral absent or mismatched+

منظوری کی تاریخ، سروس اور provider چیک کریں۔ payer اجازت دے تو correction یا appeal کریں۔

Possible causes

Missing approval, wrong service, date, provider or units.

Verification checklist

Compare authorization/referral details against billed service and payer policy.

Resolution / next action

Correct supported discrepancies; seek retro-authorization only when permitted; appeal with evidence if justified.

Follow-up

Track authorization review or appeal decision; apply contractual rules if upheld.

Sample A/R note (illustrative)

Authorization denial reviewed. Approval [status]; discrepancy [finding]. Action [action]. Ref [reference].

08Timely filing denialClaim received outside applicable filing limit+

متعلقہ filing limit اور submission evidence چیک کریں۔ ثبوت کے ساتھ اپیل کریں۔

Possible causes

Late submission, rejection, incorrect payer or missing receipt evidence.

Verification checklist

Verify plan-specific original/corrected/secondary filing rules, DOS and accepted submission history.

Resolution / next action

Appeal with valid timely receipt evidence or applicable exception; if unsupported, escalate for contractual adjustment review.

Follow-up

Track appeal deadline and determination; do not automatically bill patient.

Sample A/R note (illustrative)

TFL denial reviewed. Filing limit [rule]; receipt evidence [finding]. Appeal/adjustment review [status].

09Patient responsibilityBalance assigned to patient after adjudication+

Final EOB، secondary coverage اور billing rules چیک کریں۔ صرف درست ذمہ داری مریض کو بل کریں۔

Possible causes

Deductible, copay, coinsurance or potentially incorrect liability.

Verification checklist

Review final EOB, secondary coverage, contract, notices and applicable billing protections.

Resolution / next action

Bill only validated patient liability after required payer processing; resolve discrepancies before statements.

Follow-up

Reconcile balance and document approved disposition.

Sample A/R note (illustrative)

Final EOB reviewed. Patient liability [amount/type] validated against [rule]; secondary [status].

10Credit balance / overpaymentPayment exceeds valid account balance+

Payment history reconcile کریں۔ تصدیق شدہ overpayment کے لیے منظور شدہ refund یا recoupment process اپنائیں۔

Possible causes

Duplicate payment, reversal, COB change or posting error.

Verification checklist

Reconcile ledger, ERA, EFT/check and prior refunds or recoupments.

Resolution / next action

Correct posting errors; route confirmed overpayment through approved refund or recoupment process and applicable deadlines.

Follow-up

Verify refund or offset completion and avoid double recovery.

Sample A/R note (illustrative)

Credit balance reviewed. Cause [finding]. Refund/recoupment [status] per approved process. Follow-up [date].

11Eligibility / coverage denialCoverage not active or member data mismatch+

DOS پر coverage اور member ID verify کریں۔ درست payer کو قواعد کے مطابق کلیم بھیجیں۔

Possible causes

Wrong member ID, terminated plan or incorrect payer.

Verification checklist

Verify eligibility for DOS, demographics, plan and COB using authorized systems.

Resolution / next action

Correct verified insurance details and submit to correct payer within filing rules; escalate unresolved coverage.

Follow-up

Confirm acceptance or final coverage decision.

Sample A/R note (illustrative)

Eligibility verified for DOS; [finding]. Insurance updated only as supported. Next action [action].

12Duplicate claim denialPayer identifies a previously processed claim+

اصل کلیم اور EOB چیک کریں۔ حقیقی duplicate دوبارہ submit نہ کریں۔

Possible causes

Repeated original submission or duplicate service edit.

Verification checklist

Locate original claim and EOB; compare DOS, procedure, units and provider.

Resolution / next action

If true duplicate, reconcile original; if distinct supported service or correction, follow payer correction/dispute rules.

Follow-up

Track original or corrected claim rather than repeatedly submitting.

Sample A/R note (illustrative)

Duplicate denial reviewed; original claim [number/status]. Action [action] based on comparison.

13Bundling / modifier denialService included in another procedure+

Code pair اور documentation کا جائزہ لیں۔ صرف جائز modifier استعمال کریں۔

Possible causes

Coding edit, global package or payer bundling policy.

Verification checklist

Review code pair, documentation, applicable edits and modifier eligibility with qualified coder.

Resolution / next action

Use modifier only when supported and permitted; otherwise follow adjustment or appeal policy.

Follow-up

Track coding review and payer decision.

Sample A/R note (illustrative)

Bundling denial reviewed. Code pair [codes]; coding review [finding]. Action [action].

14Maximum benefits reachedBenefit limit exhausted+

Benefit limit اور secondary coverage چیک کریں۔ درست COB قواعد کے مطابق کارروائی کریں۔

Possible causes

Visit, frequency, dollar or service benefit limit.

Verification checklist

Verify benefit period, utilization, EOB and secondary coverage.

Resolution / next action

Dispute incorrect utilization with evidence; submit to secondary if appropriate under COB; validate patient liability separately.

Follow-up

Confirm secondary adjudication or final contractual disposition.

Sample A/R note (illustrative)

Primary denied maximum benefit. Limit [verified finding]. Secondary eligibility/COB [status]. Action [action].

15Payment issued but not postedPayer shows paid; ledger unresolved+

ERA، EFT یا check trace اور payee چیک کریں۔ finance کے ساتھ reconcile کریں۔

Possible causes

Missing ERA, EFT/check mismatch or payment sent to another recipient.

Verification checklist

Obtain remittance, payment date, trace/check number, payee and deposit status.

Resolution / next action

Coordinate with payment posting and finance; request trace or reissue when appropriate. Do not post unverified payment.

Follow-up

Reconcile deposit and remittance; document final posting.

Sample A/R note (illustrative)

Payer reports payment [amount/date], trace [ref], payee [status]. Referred for reconciliation.
